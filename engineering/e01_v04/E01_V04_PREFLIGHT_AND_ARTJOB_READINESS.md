## 6. Supported versus refused motion classes, and how to resolve each without compromising the art
| Class | Engine today | Resolution that keeps the art and direction intact |
|---|---|---|
| Gaze, idle, hold, reach, single-hand grip and release, react, change_expression, limited speak, head turn within the rigid limit | VERIFIED_CODE on diagnostic rigs (P06) | Real art plus each character's approved performance profile |
| Two hands on one prop (A02, A04) | No executor (`possessionAt` keeps one holder) | Engineering: a driving hand plus a FOLLOW hand, with a two-contact executor and sequential release, qualified on two unlike rigs |
| Two-actor handover (A08) | No producer | Engineering: a TRANSFER producer with one transform owner per instant, a dual-support window, a transfer frame and release order, and an executor proving no support gap. Restaging is not recommended: the handover is a directed beat. |
| Page held by its edge (A06, A08) | PINCH refused | Decide: direct it as a GRIP on a page-edge socket with wrapped thumb/finger layers, or build PINCH |
| Lead approach with planted feet (A06) | Articulated walk refused | Decide: an approved gait cel cycle, building articulated walk, or feet below the counter crop with a root move (an explicit user choice) |
| Gradual expression (A02, A10, A13) | Cel swaps only (raster) | Inbetween expression cels, or vector mouth/brow paths for a registered morph |
| Phoneme lip-sync (A05, A14, A17, A18, A19) | LIMITED mouth only | Approve `limited_mouth` in each speaking profile, or build visemes plus phoneme art |
| World freeze, selective colour (A20, A22) | No operator | Engineering: a compositor time-remap (source frame 959) with an executor proving every owner's pixels equal f959, plus a masked colour effect |
| Body turn (A21) | Needs approved body views | Draw the view family, or turn the head and eyes only |
| Host in the 2D room (A23–A26) | Diagnostic adapter only | Engineering: a perspective camera and depth/matte/shadow passes. Art: an approved host `.blend`. |
