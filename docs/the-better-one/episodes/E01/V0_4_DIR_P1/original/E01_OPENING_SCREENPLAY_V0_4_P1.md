## Screenplay — The Award
INT. MIA'S RESTAURANT — LUNCH (X; financial report M01). Start inside a busy room, not a logo. Every visible table occupied, a small group waits outside. Mia holds her fictional local dining award (signature-steak emblem); a regular across the counter has a phone ready.

**REGULAR:** Hold it up, Mia. Big smile.

Mia raises the plaque and exaggerates a regal chin-up pose, without mocking the award.

**MIA:** Too much?

The regular chuckles; Mia breaks into a genuine grin. The shift lead arrives as part of the ordinary daily closing-report routine, discreetly from the work aisle. The regular relaxes the phone toward the chest.

**SHIFT LEAD:** Mia? Yesterday's numbers.

**MIA:** Thanks.

She places the plaque on its stable base, releases both hands naturally and receives the single-page report with her right hand. Left arm settles free. She reads; the smile fades.

INSERT: YESTERDAY / Sales $3,210 / Operating result −$120 / LOSS. Same sheet and grip.

Back to Mia. The regular looks across the packed room, then back to her.

**REGULAR:** At this rate, you're gonna need a bigger place.

Mia lowers the report discreetly, looks up and takes a small breath.

**MIA:** Let's get through today.

She recreates her cheerful smile; the award remains on the counter within the photo, the paper below its crop. The regular raises the phone after Mia re-engages; waits for her reply to end; click. The regular lowers it to review; Mia's smile holds with worried eyes. Freeze the actual outgoing pose, not a new photograph.

**HOST (VO):** This is Mia.

Selective color; brief THIS IS MIA typography. Proposed unobtrusive label “Fictional story · illustrative financial model” before explanatory modeling. Match to the same restaurant three years earlier: Mia turns her OPEN sign, sees empty tables, waits for a first customer.

**HOST (VO):** Three years ago, she opened this place.

Return to frozen present, same props and characters.

**HOST (VO):** Now she's worried she won't make payroll.

The actual 3D host walks in through the public aisle; looks to Mia, then the crowded room and waiting group.

**HOST:** Could she just serve more people?

He considers the room. No answer yet.

**HOST:** First, let's see where the money goes.

Cut into investigation at f1950. No locked shot after this boundary; later Mia supplies operational knowledge, and first real value remains due by 90–120s.
