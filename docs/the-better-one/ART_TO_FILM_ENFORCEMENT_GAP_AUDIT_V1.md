## Important truths and open gaps

- **296/296 is a valid reported software regression but does not prove F01–F80 paintings look professional or that they animate naturally.**
- **14/14 diagnostic effects is not 14 production-capable verbs for every character.** The report distinguishes 12 supported under tested rigs, walk refused without approved cycle and body turn refused without approved view family.
- **The manual operator loop exists in executable form,** including ZIP export, candidate selection, intake, SHA-scoped approvals and root-cause repair learning. What did not yet exist as one operational document was the artistic and production QA protocol; ART_TO_FINISHED_FILM_OPERATING_STANDARD_V1.md now defines it. Claude still must test actual E01 job dispatch and visual feedback integration.
- **The art model may not create independent transparent editable layers as reliably as a hand-drawn production source.** If actual image bytes do not include the exact separate painted part, occluded areas, masks and registered view states, a generic "same character" prompt or cropping a flattened image is NOT a real fix. It needs source-attached regeneration, art reconstruction approved by a human, or a different approved animation method.
- **E01 V0.4 is still proposed.** A provisional source-level map can proceed now; sealed official ArtJob ZIPs requiring real signed story/direction/design must not be fabricated.
- **The production docs and tested P06 source are on separate branches.** A docs-only merge is needed and not by itself evidence that the stage-to-stage runtime is connected for real images.
- **No accepted genuine film seconds, no approved Mia master, no approved Blender host .blend, no complete measured word audio.** Those are external creative production tasks, not failures to be masked with diagnostic assets.
