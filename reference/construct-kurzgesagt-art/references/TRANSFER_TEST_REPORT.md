## Observations from actual pixels, not inferred from prompts

1. **Text-only high-detail directions are not sufficient visual control.** They can be executed into attractive images that follow the semantic story yet use a materially different illustration dialect. This was observed in A and especially B.
2. **Style-image witness conditioning materially changes the visual result**, in this test bringing simpler mass-based shading and more source-like face/surface/plant treatment. It does not establish the counterfactual with only one controlled variable, because C also tightened text constraints; the relative contribution of each is unmeasured.
3. **Source identity leakage is a risk**, especially human accessories/hair and visual arrangements. A style transfer can look familiar because it copies distinctive source character traits; that is a disqualifying solution to the generalization goal.
4. Six simultaneous **illustrated alternate states** can preserve a guided new character's recognizable identity at sheet scale (F), but this is not the same as temporally coherent animation, consistent pose interpolation, audio/transition timing or arbitrary-script reliability.
5. Strongest demonstrated partial transfer: large contiguous fills, simple locally attached color regions, contrast/foreground depth in C and D, and six guided views/poses in F. Weakest evidence: unsupervised generation of one canonical identity in many independent requests and source-style moving-film motion.
6. The ArtJob compiler was executed on a fully specified novel scene and its **full** compiled payload was submitted in E. The automated trace was emitted before submission and still says `COMPILED_NOT_DEMONSTRATED`; it intentionally does not claim it received a provider output. The additional resulting image is evidence in this report; automated post-provider receipt capture and independent per-rule pixel checking are not yet integrated into the compiler.

## What would qualify this skill

- Frozen construction-method version and prompt/compiler trace; real provider request and output receipt for each run.
- Held-out original briefs from distinct unfamiliar material families; multiple candidates not cherry-picked from retries.
- At least one full new identity with front, three-quarter, profile, expressive face, gripping hands and multiple body poses, all using the same canonical identity without manual per-scene drawing repairs.
- At least one rich new world whose perspective, lighting state, environment density and contacts remain coherent through camera changes.
- A moving original sequence with character+prop contact, simultaneous motion, a visual-state transition and audio cues; no arbitrary still-image slideshow masquerading as animation.
- Independent blind review comparing source-like artistic construction, not subject copying, with detailed pass/fail per style subsystem. Source images must be sampled for relevant counterexamples.
- Causal ablation: remove/alter an actual learned shape/shadow/contact rule from the compiled provider job and confirm an expected, observable difference in output.
- Failure-driven repair that changes a transferable method and improves a distinct held-out result, not a one-scene hand patch.
