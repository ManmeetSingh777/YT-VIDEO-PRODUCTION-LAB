# Five entire MP4s: longitudinal atlas, source partitions, method witnesses

Study date: 2026-11-19. Source provenance is the user's five complete 1920×1080, 60-fps MP4 files. The five original durations total **4035.233378 s** and the video-stream counters report **242,095 frames**. See the content-hash registry in `FULL_FILM_SOURCE_REGISTRY.json`; the accompanying scratch study contains reproducible 1-Hz timeline atlases and native-resolution witness extraction code. This adds coverage of the full time extents, **not** semantic inspection of all 242,095 full-HD frames. Earlier granular work remains fifteen native-frame intervals; this extension adds **43 visually inspected atlas pages** (~one thumbnail per second, including advertising and credits) and **66 selected native-resolution frames** across **22 independent temporal method strips**. One-second and three-still strips cannot prove subsecond easing, frame-by-frame topology, audio/voice rhythm or a complete moving-film production technique.

## Reading the provenance codes

`FILM@seconds` identifies an approximate timestamp in the corresponding complete MP4. `A` means the one-second thumbnail atlas was inspected. `N` means selected original-resolution 1920×1080 frames were obtained at exact listed seconds and inspected (some with short before/after strips). `M` is a proposed reusable **method**, not a source fact. `AD` is a clearly separate sponsor/product/merch/footer section. Timestamp boundaries are approximate where the video cuts mid-second; they are navigation ranges, not frame-accurate editorial EDLs. For fine motion, go to the original MP4 by timestamp and inspect a denser frame strip.

## Cross-film invariants backed by *unlike* context

1. **Contiguous parent surfaces own their details.** Compare `NUCLEAR@51` (multilayer fly material), `MEMORIES@75` (bounded synaptic membrane and vesicles), `AI@250–252` (separate case panels) and existing scene-native character eyes. The transferable operation is create parent mask, local subplanes, nested parts and correct occlusion, **not** reuse fly or neuron contours.
2. **The same film contains opposed render modes.** `BRAIN@1.6–4.4` preserves scene geometry under explicit perception-effect remapping; `AI@356` puts a simple diagram on flat cyan; `AI@842` resolves a multi-plane character in dark dimension; `MEMORIES@62–64` may have extraordinary microcomponent density. Thus blanket rules for detail density, palette, black lines or blur are contradicted.
3. **Time can change the whole world state.** `NUCLEAR@384/387/390` shows daylight/twilight/night states changing sky, lighting devices, window emissions, roof highlights, distant mountains and vehicle tones coherently; `BRAIN@306–308` shows separate pose/ghost trajectories. A one-LUT recolor or one puppet translation cannot reproduce those modes.
4. **Information graphics are modeled, not decorated.** `AI@356–366` incremental nodes-and-links, `NUCLEAR@296–298` region/movement, `SOLAR@450–455` time/distance measure, `MEMORIES@140–145` cortical separation; encode nodes, paths, labels, axis, spatial order, reveal states. Symbols must have corresponding narration/meaning or be removed.
5. **Contained scenes are independently complete worlds.** `MEMORIES@464–466` shows a scene with ground, horizon, three actors and depth inside a shell-shaped screen; `BRAIN` uses framed/subframed viewpoints, and AI includes computer displays. The shell/crop and scene have separate layers/coordinate systems. Never treat an empty colored orb with random particles as a reusable container recipe.
6. **Production has medium-dependent exceptions.** Narrative vector-like fillings coexist with procedural micro-trees, composited glow, blur, scanline/recording viewports, UI rendering, cel/exaggerated state alternates, and photographs within sponsor/merch. F19's conditional production-medium choice is reinforced, not replaced.

## Explicit nonconclusions and blind spots

- **No** claim to have artistically inspected all 242,095 native video frames. Whole-file decoding to generate the 1-Hz atlas is a computational traversal, not all-frame semantic perception. 66 new full-HD sampled stills supplement the earlier sampled 3,492 low-res witnesses.
- A repeated color in one film is not a universal HEX rule. Character heads are not all the same template, and an extreme-expression thick outline does not imply global outlines.
- The atlas is not a transcript, score or animation curve; no voice cadence, sound effects, easing curve, compositing graph, exact proprietary authoring software or full artwork identity has been recovered from MP4s.
- `A` and `N` observations justify method **candidates**. Exact faithful replication and motion/audio transfer require independently designed novel scripts, moving clips, renderer control and blinded comparisons. See `FULL_FILM_ROUTER_AND_METHODS.md` and `TRANSFER_TEST_REPORT.md`.
