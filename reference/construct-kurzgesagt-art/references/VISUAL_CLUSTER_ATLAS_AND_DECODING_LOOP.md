# Visual-system cluster atlas and evidence-directed question loop

**Active priority:** use this document before choosing a source witness or constructing an original unfamiliar-script image. It is additive to F01–F56. It corrects the recurrent failure in which an agent reads broad visual adjectives, generates a painterly still, and then relies on cosmetic prompt revisions. The objects of study are **executable visual causes**, not source scenes.

## 1. Exact coverage and epistemic boundary

The five supplied source films contain **242,095 encoded frames** at 1920×1080 and 60 fps: AI 60,169; BRAIN 45,188; MEMORIES 50,147; NUCLEAR 40,782; SOLAR 45,809. The complete original-resolution *numerical* YUV/luminance temporal pass includes every encoded frame and has its own original source-scan receipts. It did not interpret the semantic content of every image. The new visually inspectable survey covers all **4,035 second positions** in source-native JPEGs, partitions all chronology into **2,260 contiguous units** of at most 120 encoded frames, groups those sampled stills into **110 automatically generated appearance clusters** (22 per film), numerically profiles **324 native representative frames**, and studies 9 separate 3-second transitions at 6 fps (**162 native images**). Those are complementary forms of evidence, **not 242,095 individual artistic judgments**.

The portable subset in `assets/question_native_witnesses/` is **25 personally selected full-HD counterexample frames**, with exact timestamp and SHA-256 in `QUESTION_NATIVE_WITNESS_MANIFEST.json`. These are positively inspected source witnesses, not a semantic label for the entire unviewed original sequence. Use them alongside the older 19+8 anchors. The scratch-wide 4,035 JPEG corpus, 110-cluster boards and 162 temporal frames are research backing; **not all are portable into a fresh chat**. If a deciding high-impact observation needs one of these not installed with the skill, retrieve that original frame rather than fabricating a claim.

**Editorial contamination:** all five timelines also contain promotional/sponsor/editorial artwork. At least BRAIN contains a Steam advertisement and SOLAR a Saily live-action/promo insertion; NUCLEAR contains promotional educational screens; AI and MEMORIES also have distinct non-explainer inserts. A bright/high-edge computer-vision cluster can mix these with genuine explainer art. **Never infer a core studio-illustration rule from an unclassified ad, sponsor screenshot, real photograph, logo or product UI.** Before promoting an anchor, classify it `EXPLAINER_DRAWING`, `EXPLAINER_COMPOSITE`, `EDITORIAL_PROMO`, `LIVE_ACTION`, or `UNRESOLVED`.

A mark with no owner is either removed or given a new justified subfamily. An unfamiliar source observation that belongs to none of A–F creates a new subfamily, and the atlas must be revised; a model must not solve that with an adjective.

## 3. High-impact question selection rule

The *next* question is selected by expected reduction in whole-image failure, not by the easiest detail to measure. For each candidate issue assess four independent factors on a 0–3 ordinal scale (these weights are **engineering priorities**, not source measurements):

- `G`: fraction of rendered image/film affected; 3 means a whole-world or entire focal subject error.
- `C`: causal comprehension damage; 3 means viewer can no longer reconstruct the explained relationship.
- `R`: recurrence across unlike future content; 3 means common across character, biological, mechanical and abstract worlds.
- `U`: uncertainty/contradiction in currently cited source evidence; 3 means two unlike source samples actively disagree with a claimed universal rule.

Sort descending by `4*C + 3*G + 3*R + 2*U` (heuristic). If an issue has a wrong causal mechanism, prioritize it over local color polish **even when it has fewer pixels**. Do not treat these numbers as object-style parameters. At each loop, ask **“Which unobserved condition would reverse my rule?”** and deliberately inspect a source that tests that reversal.

### Mandatory seven-move question loop

1. **Reproduce the failure:** actual generated image or missing operation. State one detectable error, e.g. `full-frame pictorial environment when the inference needed one actor and one relation`.
2. **Ask a discriminating question:** `Under what source-visual condition is a large environmental field the causal proof rather than decoration?` Do not ask merely `how are backgrounds rich?`.
3. **Inspect opposing original-native contexts:** select at least one confirming and one *genuinely unlike* counterexample. Keep exact film/timestamp and evidence class. Revisit 6fps or every encoded frame of the relevant transition when the change between two sampled seconds is disputed.
4. **Measure the actual visual operation:** named silhouettes, contact endpoints, screen-relative bboxes, region-owned colors, occluder coverage, spatial axes, number and ownership of repeat motifs, correspondence landmarks, before/after identity, etc. Record observable vs interpretive attributes separately.
5. **State a conditional producing rule:** `IF image must establish geographic coverage THEN allow an aerial field; implement river/forest as substrate-owned color regions and moving aircraft/action overlay; ELSE do not show aerial terrain`. Include a falsifier and prohibited counter-default.
6. **Execute on unrelated new subject:** construct and submit an image-provider request with the actual unrelated witnesses attached, inspect pixels, and change an upstream rule on failure. Distinguish FIRST_PASS from REPAIR. For motion, test registered assets/temporal proof, not only a still.
7. **Close or continue:** a rule is `SOURCE_OBSERVED`, then `METHOD_CANDIDATE`, then `OUTPUT_DEMONSTRATED`, then `TRANSFER_TESTED`; never mark `QUALIFIED` from compiled text. If missing structural evidence or an output failure remains, log the exact unknown and ask a new discriminating question immediately. Do not output a “finished” label merely because a frame census exists.

Use `MISSING_SOURCE` and `NOT_TESTED` explicitly; do not fill them with fictional observations. Each selected operation must tie to a **new** subject graph, never a reference's literal narrative.
