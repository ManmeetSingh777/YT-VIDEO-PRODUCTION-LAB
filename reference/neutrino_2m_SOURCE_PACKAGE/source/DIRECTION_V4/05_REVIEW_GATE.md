# Quality gates: observable evidence, not self-scores

The gate scores are judgment calls until evaluated by viewers; tests cannot mathematically certify artistic quality. These conditions are early stop signals for obvious failure.

## Gate A — factual and narrative
- One immediate visual and spoken hook; viewer knows *what question the film answers* by ~12 s.
- Scientific claims checked against the attached source notes; the IceCube flashes are from secondary particles, neutrinos aren't visibly bright, and Earth isn't perfectly transparent to *all* neutrino energies.
- An unfamiliar viewer can paraphrase the cause-and-effect chain in 3 sentences; narration follows visual events.

## Gate B — artistic quality
- Five 1920×1080 ACTUAL frames, each has layered depth and unique design work, not placeholder boxes, stock imagery, three basic concentric circles, or flat labels.
- Same original character and neutrino symbol are identifiable across scenes. Compositions are readable at thumbnail size.
- Changing geography and scale are visually convincing rather than flat background scale-up.
- Internal art direction judges against professional illustrative craft, explicitly reports where it falls short.

## Gate C — real animation
- A genuinely connected 12-second proof (not frozen hero frames with camera pans) includes anticipation, character response, secondary motion, source-to-world transformation, occlusion and parallax.
- No frame jumps or camera jitter. Test actual video at normal speed.
- Motion is connected to a narrative event rather than filling time.

## Gate D — full two minutes
- Final duration 120 seconds (tolerance ±0.3 s); 1920x1080; 30fps; playable MP4 with actual audio if available; no black unintended frames.
- Narration aligned to events and not rushed or absent without explicit report; no generic endless title slides; no massive unmotivated dead holds.
- Edited scene handoffs preserve cause and identity. First and last images connect emotionally.
- Honest summary of actual remaining defects. External cold-viewer review still needed to claim professional-level output.

## Important non-promises
This test cannot *guarantee* 20-minute reproducibility, zero manual feedback, a particular dollar cost or equivalence with a specific studio. But it should produce valuable, inspectable artifacts rather than another speculative architecture.
