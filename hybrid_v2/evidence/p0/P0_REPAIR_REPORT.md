## Defects found while proving the repairs (fixed at the producer)
- `svg_parts.mjs#emitSpritePart`: the `asset_to_canonical` wrapper was emitted in reversed order. SVG applies transform lists right-to-left, so registered parts with non-identity transforms were displaced: the arm floated above the head in the first round-trip render. Fixed, with a regression assertion and a rendered landmark pixel probe (all rig landmarks land on painted art at frames 99, 100 and 101).
- Two-landmark part registration could never detect a wrong landmark (a 4-DOF fit through 2 points has zero residual). It now needs ≥3.
- `approveState` checked only re-bound slots; it now covers every visible asset. State approval bundles include the default slots.
- Refused registration attempts overwrote the accepted receipt; each attempt now has its own folder.
- The hv2 proposal's own contract errors, exposed by the graph-derived planner:
  - a front-view smile that the profile view always hides (now a registered `profile_satisfied` state with profile expression art);
  - the "hand_open" state held through the grip (now closed on contact, open on release).
  The S02 staging stays feasible.
