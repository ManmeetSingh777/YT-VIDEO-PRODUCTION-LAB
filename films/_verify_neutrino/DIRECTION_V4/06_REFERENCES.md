# Researched production precedents and how to use them

1. Official Kurzgesagt process: https://kurzgesagt.org/what-we-do
   They research a sourced scientific question, develop the script, SKETCH visual metaphors, illustrate with vector/pixel art, animate to recorded voiceover, then soundtrack and mix. **Lesson:** narration timing and explanatory visual metaphor come BEFORE ornamentation.

2. Official Kurzgesagt production overview: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uFk0mgljtns
   High-volume custom illustration, animation and production are highly labor intensive. **Lesson:** don't pretend two minutes of finished high-end animation is one simple prompt; automate separate artistic responsibilities, inspect rendered outcomes.

3. Kurzgesagt official Black Hole storyboard poster: https://shop-eu.kurzgesagt.org/products/storyboard-poster-largest-blackhole
   An example of visually specific successive shots in a single edited story, not a single visual style image. **Lesson:** reference materials should include series of connected beats and visual contrast, not only stills.

4. Disney Animation storyboard process: https://disneyanimation.com/process/story/
   Story artists visualize emotion, timing, staging, framing in a sequence of drawings. **Lesson:** force visual evidence, not long elegant prose.

5. Disney Animation layout: https://disneyanimation.com/process/layout/
   Camera choice, actor staging, rough motion and story continuity all tested shot by shot. **Lesson:** ensure camera/motion direction changes what a viewer understands.

6. Disney Animation: https://www.disneyanimation.com/process/animation/
   Timing, staging, anticipation, follow through, squash/stretch and secondary action bring characters to life. **Lesson:** animate acting and object reaction, not universal eased translations.

7. HyperFrames official prompting/technical contracts: https://hyperframes.app/docs/3-guides/2-prompting
   `/hyperframes` skill, registered GSAP timelines, timed clips, seeded random numbers and render check. **Lesson:** respect actual engine mechanics rather than asking for React/other engine outputs.

IceCube quick facts https://icecube.wisc.edu/about-us/facts/ , Fermilab https://neutrinos.fnal.gov/sources/solar-neutrinos/ , NASA https://science.nasa.gov/universe/glossary/ ; IceCube Cherenkov signal https://icecube.wisc.edu/news/research/2022/10/newly-discovered-optical-effect-allows-icecube-to-deduce-ice-crystal-properties/
   Scientific grounding. See `01_SCRIPT_AND_FACTS.md` for constraints.

9. Limited third-party sample of Kurzgesagt SHORT-FORM posts: https://www.reelpantry.com/teardown/kurz_gesagt
   Measured average shots about every 3.5 seconds across **eight TikTok clips**. **Lesson:** energetic editing inspires frequent meaningful visual progress; do not assert this is a measured cadence for all long-form films.

10. Multimedia-learning research: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5132380/
    Visual signalling, voice/graphic alignment and restrained extraneous detail help explanation. **Lesson:** keep focal events readable even with rich illustration.

These are technical/filmmaking references, not assets to copy. Do not download copyrighted vector panels, scenes, audio or mascots to reuse in the new film.
