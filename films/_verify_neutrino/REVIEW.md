## Known shortcomings
- **Voice:** `NOT FINAL AUDIO QUALITY`. Kokoro af_heart, local TTS, sentences spliced with set gaps. Sentence timing is real; word timing inside sentences is estimated by ear-free proportion, so some on-word landings may be slightly off.
- **Music and SFX:** procedural, generated by `tools/audio.py`. Measured, never heard. Likely the weakest craft in the film.
- **Character:** front-facing rig with sliding face features; no true 3/4 or rear views as the brief asked. Hands are simple.
- **Art density** falls after the opening: the lattice, the question-mark stage and the data view are cleaner and sparser than the kitchen or solar core. IceCube strings read as a fairly regular grid rather than deep perspective.
- **Sensor layout** is illustrative, not the real IceCube geometry. Muon speed is slowed for legibility, and hit colours are illustrative of arrival time.
- **Rare dim beat:** about 0.5 s of near-empty dark frame near 99 s as the data dots begin.
- **Transitions:** scene changes use cloud wipes, fades and hard cuts as well as matched moves; not every handoff is a continuous object transformation. The window-glint-to-Sun transition in the brief became window-to-city-to-Earth-to-Sun.
- **Sun:** the surface at full frame is still a stylised granule pattern; the core ribbons are decorative rather than a physical model.
- **Shot map:** adapted, not followed. 3D not used; a hero-pose ride on the neutrino track and the scientists digging beat were simplified.
- **Timing:** hero times follow the voice, so the Earth hero is at 0:50 and IceCube at 1:34 rather than 0:56 and 1:34 from the map.
